# Vision

yaai.os ("yet another ai os") is a home OS: the homepage of your media center. You turn on the TV and land on your
own things, not on someone else's catalogue.

## What it is

- The first screen of the media center and the place you return to.
- It shows what the household chose to put there: its media, its apps, its shortcuts.
- It carries no ads and no unrelated suggested content, anywhere in the product (INV-PRODUCT-001). Content appears
  because a person in the household asked for it, not because a provider wants it seen.

## Who it is for

A household that watches, listens and plays on one shared screen, driven from a remote, a keyboard, a gamepad or a
touch screen.

## Not decided yet

- The features beyond being the homepage of the media center.
- The architecture: which devices it runs on, how it is built, how it reaches media and apps.
- What role AI plays in the product.

Each of these becomes a doc here, an ADR in [[adr/SKILL]], or a screen doc in [[screens/SKILL]] once the user decides
it. Until then, treat them as open and ask.
